Privacy Policy

Account and Google Sign-In

When you sign in with Google, Google shares your account identifier, email address and basic profile information permitted on its consent screen. Classroom Translate uses this information to create or recognize your account, sign you in and associate your account with synced classroom materials. Supabase processes the sign-in and stores account records. On iPhone and iPad, Classroom Translate stores its sign-in session in the device’s Keychain.

If you choose Sign in with Apple where available, Apple supplies the sign-in credentials used by the Classroom account service.

Classroom recordings and sync

The microphone is used when you start listening to or recording a class. The original recording is kept on the device. Supported captions and online study notes can sync with your Classroom account. Recording sync can send copies to your own iCloud with Plus or to Classroom cloud storage with Pro, where those features are available. You can manage classroom sync and recording sync separately in the app.

Translation and AI

On-device transcription, translation and supported AI features process the material on your device. Online translation, caption review and online study notes send relevant caption text and context to OpenAI to perform the requested task. Online AI questions may also send the question you enter and its supporting context. Google account details are used for sign-in. If your classroom content includes personal information, that information can be part of the content sent for an online feature. You can turn off online translation in the app’s translation settings.

Access and deletion

Use the app to manage saved classroom materials and account settings. To request deletion, open the personal account section in Settings and select the option to delete your Classroom account. This deletes the account and the associated Classroom data. Copies you exported or independently stored, including your own iCloud copies, can be managed separately. For access requests or help with deletion, contact us using the email below.

Contact

For privacy questions, email classroomtranslate@gmail.com.
