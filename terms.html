Classroom use

Classroom Translate helps you follow spoken lessons and review saved classroom materials. Follow school or classroom rules and get any permission required before recording, transcribing or sharing a class. Use only material you are allowed to provide, and respect other people’s privacy and rights.

Captions and translations

Automatic captions and translations can miss or misinterpret speech. For important instructions, dates and figures, check the original material or confirm with the speaker.
